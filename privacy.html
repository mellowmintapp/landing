Mellowmint Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Closet Flow handles information. Closet Flow works offline, and signed-in accounts can sync data across devices.

1. Information Closet Flow Handles

  • Account information: Email or Google sign-in is used to authenticate your account.
  • Closet and outfit data: Clothing details, brands, categories, saved outfits, and related photos are stored on your device. When you sign in, this data and outfit changes sync to the cloud.
  • Location and weather: If you grant location permission while using the app, Closet Flow uses your location to request current weather for outfit suggestions. Weather results are cached on your device for offline use.
  • Product links: If you import an item from a supported retailer link, the app uses that link to retrieve product details.
  • Optional AI tagging: Pro AI occasion tagging is available only when the feature is configured. It processes a tagging request through a cloud function.

2. Storage and Service Providers

Closet Flow stores app data locally so core features can work offline. For signed-in users, account and app data sync through Supabase services, including authentication, database, storage, and real-time updates. Weather requests and optional AI tagging are handled by cloud functions. These services process information to provide the features described above.

3. Location Permission

Location access is used only while the app is in use to obtain weather for outfit suggestions. You can change or revoke location permission in your device settings; weather-based suggestions may then be unavailable.

4. Account and Data Deletion

Closet Flow provides an in-app account deletion option. Use it from within the app to delete your account. App data saved only on a device can also be removed by deleting it in the app or removing the app from that device.

5. Other MellowMint Apps

Memory by Seasons and Fun with Crosswords may handle information differently. This policy does not describe their app-specific data practices; refer to the privacy disclosures provided with each app.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our apps and services change. Updates will be posted here and reflected in the relevant app store listings.
