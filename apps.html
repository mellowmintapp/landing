Get more from what you already own

  • A photo-led closet with colors, brands, categories, and occasion tags
  • Build and save looks, log outfit wears, and rediscover forgotten combinations
  • Favorite items, outfits, brands, and categories with quick-access filters
  • Wardrobe insights to explore your palette and spot outfit gaps
  • Work offline; cloud sync stays free when you sign in
  • Add photos or import from supported product links; not every retailer is supported

Start free. Grow into Pro.

Free: Up to 50 active clothing items and 5 saved outfits, the outfit builder, favorites, search, signed-in sync, and daily or weekly reminders.

Pro: Room for your whole wardrobe, plus help deciding what to wear today.

  • Unlimited clothing items and saved outfits
  • Today’s weather and weather-matched outfit suggestions from your closet
  • Occasion ideas informed by your wardrobe and weather
  • Active Stylist morning and evening reminders with custom times

Closet Flow Pro: $4.99/month or $29.99/year. Final pricing and any trial eligibility are shown in the app store purchase screen.

Reminder messages refresh when you open the app. Morning weather nudges may use your recent weather check; they are not live forecasts.
