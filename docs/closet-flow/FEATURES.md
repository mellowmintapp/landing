# Closet Flow

Closet Flow helps you see what you own, organize your wardrobe, and decide what to wear. It is designed for iOS and Android, with local storage for offline use and cloud sync for signed-in accounts.

## What you can do

- **Organize your closet:** Add clothing, shoes, and accessories with photos, categories, brands, garment colors, sizes, and notes.
- **Find your pieces:** Search your wardrobe and browse by category, brand, season, color, occasion, or favorites where available.
- **Keep favorites close:** Favorite items, outfits, brands, and categories, then revisit them from your dashboard.
- **Build and save looks:** Choose an occasion, combine your own pieces, customize the result, and save outfits to wear again.
- **Get more from your wardrobe:** Log outfit wears, rediscover saved looks, and explore wardrobe insights.
- **Set outfit reminders:** Choose optional daily or weekly reminders at your preferred time.
- **Sync across devices:** Sign in to sync your closet and saved outfits. Offline changes sync when a connection is available.
- **Get weather-aware help with Pro:** Use weather-aware suggestions and occasion ideas, plus morning and evening reminders.

## Free and Pro

| Feature | Free | Pro |
|---------|------|-----|
| Active closet items | Up to 50 | Unlimited |
| Saved outfits | Up to 5 | Unlimited |
| Outfit building, search, favorites, and wardrobe insights | Included | Included |
| Signed-in cloud sync | Included | Included |
| Daily or weekly outfit reminders | Included | Included |
| Weather-aware suggestions and occasion ideas | Not included | Included |
| Morning and evening reminders | Not included | Included |
| Subscription | Free | $4.99/month or $29.99/year |

The store purchase screen confirms final pricing, billing terms, and any trial eligibility. After Pro expires, existing items and saved outfits remain accessible; Free limits apply to new additions.

## Good to know

- Product-link import supports selected retailers, not every website or product.
- Outfit suggestions are guidance, not a guarantee of weather or occasion suitability.
- Weather needs a connection for fresh results. Cached weather is not a live forecast.
- Reminders are local device notifications; delivery depends on permissions and device settings.
- Camera, photo, location, and notification access are requested for the relevant features.
- In-app help explains the main flows. Profile includes account deletion and subscription management options.
